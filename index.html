    The last page

    from Arya

    Dear Shreya,
    You’ve reached the end, or so it seems,
    but life’s a cycle, as are dreams.
    Those minute cryptics I loved of late,
    you didn’t catch on? Oh, just wait.
    Five little stars hide out of sight,
    so travel back and bring them to light.
    Solve every one, line after line,
    the very last page is yours (and mine).

    Happy 21st, My love.

    A secret, just for you
